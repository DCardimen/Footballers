## The short version

Running It Back does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal information. There are no
accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your career lives only on your device.

## What the game stores, and where

* **Your save** — your player, careers, settings and progress — is stored **only on your device**, in the app's
  local storage (and, in the iOS/Android app, the app's private preferences storage, as a safety copy). A few
  earlier copies of your save are kept on the device as automatic backups so you can undo a mistake.
* We (the developer) never receive this data. It is not uploaded anywhere.
* **Exporting a save** (Settings › Save File & Backups) creates a file you choose where to put. What happens to
  that file afterwards is up to you.

## Network use

* **iOS and Android app:** the game runs entirely from files inside the app. It makes no network requests.
* **Web version:** your browser downloads the game from [HOSTING, e.g. GitHub Pages] and checks one file
  (`rib-build.json`) to see whether a newer version has been published. That request contains no personal
  information. The host may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) under its own privacy policy:
  [LINK TO HOST'S PRIVACY POLICY, e.g. https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement].
  After your first visit the game is stored by your browser so it can be played offline.

## What we do not do

* No account or sign-in.
* No advertising, advertising identifiers or tracking across apps or websites.
* No analytics or crash-reporting services.
* No in-app purchases.
* No access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location.

## Children

The game is suitable for general audiences and collects no personal information from anyone, including children
under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country).

## Deleting your data

Use Settings › Erase All Progress, clear the site's data in your browser, or uninstall the app. Because nothing is
held on a server, there is nothing else for us to delete.
